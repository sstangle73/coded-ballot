coded-ballot

Elections for clubs and member organisations: one coded ballot per member, used once by post, online or in person.

  • Vote online (use the link from your email)
  • Check your ballot arrived, and see results
  • Check an organisation's voting page is genuine
  • Tellers and issuers
  • Issue ballots (issuers: open it from the tellers' app, with the Issue ballots button)
